# All-round Athapaththu Takes Sri Lanka 1-0 Up Against West Indies

## Athapaththu's Stellar Performance

Chamari Athapaththu proved to be the backbone of the Sri Lankan innings, scoring a crucial 45 runs off just 58 balls. Her innings was marked by a mix of aggression and caution, as she expertly navigated the West Indies bowling attack. Athapaththu's ability to rotate the strike and find boundaries at crucial junctures kept the scoreboard ticking, providing a solid foundation for her team.

But her contributions didn’t stop there. Athapaththu’s performance with the ball was equally impressive, as she claimed 3 wickets for just 18 runs in her 7 overs. Her bowling was instrumental in dismantling the West Indies batting order, which struggled to cope with the pressure exerted by the Sri Lankan bowlers.

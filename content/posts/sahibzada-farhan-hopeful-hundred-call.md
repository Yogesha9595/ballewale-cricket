# Sahibzada Farhan 'Hopeful' of Hundred Call Amid Uncertainty Around Pakistani Involvement

As the cricketing world gears up for the next season of The Hundred, Pakistani cricketer Sahibzada Farhan has expressed optimism about potentially being selected for the tournament. This comes at a time when the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) has sent a reminder to franchises about the importance of merit-based selections, raising questions about the involvement of Pakistani players in the competition.

## Sahibzada Farhan: A Rising Star

Sahibzada Farhan has been making waves in the cricketing circuit with his impressive performances in domestic cricket. The 26-year-old opener has showcased his talent in various formats, particularly in T20 matches, where his explosive batting style has caught the attention of selectors and fans alike. With a solid average and a penchant for big scores, Farhan is seen as a valuable addition to any franchise team.

In recent interviews, Farhan expressed his eagerness to participate in The Hundred, stating, "I have been working hard on my game, and I believe I can contribute significantly to any team. The Hundred is a fantastic platform, and I hope to get the opportunity to showcase my skills on such a prestigious stage."

## ECB's Reminder: A Focus on Merit

The ECB's recent communication to The Hundred franchises has sparked discussions about player selection criteria. The board emphasized that players should be picked based on their performance and merit rather than other factors. This move aims to ensure that the tournament remains competitive and showcases the best talent available.

With the rise of various domestic leagues worldwide, the ECB's reminder serves as a timely intervention to maintain the integrity of The Hundred. It also raises questions about the inclusion of international players, particularly from Pakistan, who have faced challenges in securing spots in overseas leagues due to political and diplomatic tensions.

## The Uncertain Landscape for Pakistani Players

The involvement of Pakistani cricketers in The Hundred has been a topic of debate, especially in light of recent geopolitical developments. While the talent pool in Pakistan is vast, the selection of players has often been influenced by external factors. The ECB's commitment to merit-based selection is a positive step, but it remains to be seen how it will impact the inclusion of Pakistani players.

Farhan's hopeful stance reflects the aspirations of many Pakistani cricketers who aim to showcase their skills on international platforms. "We have a lot of talented players in Pakistan, and I believe that if given the chance, we can prove ourselves in The Hundred and other leagues," he added.

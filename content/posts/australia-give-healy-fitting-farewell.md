# Australia Give Healy Fitting Farewell with Ten-Wicket Demolition of India

In a remarkable display of dominance, the Australian women's cricket team bid farewell to legendary wicketkeeper Alyssa Healy with a resounding ten-wicket victory over India in the second Test of the series. The match, held at the iconic Sydney Cricket Ground, concluded in just over two days, highlighting Australia’s prowess in the longest format of the game. 

## Healy's Farewell: A Tribute to a Legend

Alyssa Healy, who has been a cornerstone of Australian cricket for over a decade, played her final match in front of a packed crowd, leaving an indelible mark on the sport. Known for her explosive batting and sharp wicketkeeping skills, Healy has been instrumental in numerous victories for Australia. The team’s emphatic win served as a fitting tribute to her illustrious career, as teammates and fans celebrated her contributions to women's cricket.

## Rawal's Resilience: A Bright Spot for India

Her performance was a silver lining for the Indian side, which struggled to find form throughout the match. ## Australia’s Bowling: A Masterclass in Precision

Australia's bowlers were relentless, dismantling the Indian batting lineup with precision and skill. The bowling attack, led by Megan Schutt and Ellyse Perry, proved too hot to handle for the visitors. Schutt's ability to swing the ball and Perry's all-round capabilities kept the Indian batswomen on the back foot, leading to quick dismissals. The Australian bowlers’ relentless pressure ensured that India could only muster a modest total, setting the stage for a swift conclusion to the Test.

## Voll and Litchfield: Chasing Down the Target

With a meager target of just 25 runs to chase, it was a straightforward task for the Australian openers, Georgia Voll and Phoebe Litchfield. The duo made quick work of the run chase, finishing the match in just 4.5 overs. Voll's aggressive stroke play and Litchfield's calm approach ensured that Australia cruised to victory without losing a wicket. Their partnership not only sealed the win but also demonstrated the depth of talent in the Australian batting lineup.

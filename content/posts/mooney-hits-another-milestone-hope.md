# Mooney Hits Another Milestone with Hope of 'Plenty More'

In a thrilling start to the ODI series against India, Australian opener Beth Mooney showcased her remarkable cricketing prowess, guiding her team to victory with a composed and calculated innings. ## Mooney's Steady Hand in Australia's Chase

In the first ODI of the series, played at the picturesque WACA Ground in Perth, Beth Mooney's innings was a masterclass in patience and technique. Chasing a target of 250 runs, Australia faced early challenges, but Mooney's steady presence at the crease provided the stability the team needed. Her innings of 85 runs off 102 balls was marked by elegant stroke play and strategic shot selection, allowing her to anchor the innings while rotating the strike effectively.

Mooney's ability to assess the match situation and play according to the demands of the game has been a hallmark of her career. Her innings included 8 boundaries, and she displayed an impressive range of shots, from deft cuts to powerful drives. As the wickets fell around her, Mooney's calm demeanor ensured that Australia remained on course for victory.

## A Career Milestone

With her performance in the first ODI, Mooney not only led her team to a crucial win but also reached a significant career milestone. This innings marked her 3000th run in One Day Internationals, a testament to her consistency and skill over the years. Speaking after the match, Mooney reflected on her journey, stating, "It's a special moment to reach 3000 runs, but I hope there are plenty more milestones to come. I’m just focused on contributing to the team's success."

Mooney's career has been nothing short of remarkable. Since her debut in 2016, she has established herself as one of the leading players in women's cricket. Her ability to perform under pressure, coupled with her tactical acumen, makes her an invaluable asset to the Australian team.

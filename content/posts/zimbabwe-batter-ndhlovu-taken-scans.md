# Zimbabwe Batter Ndhlovu Taken for Scans After Being Hit by Illing Bouncer

In a concerning turn of events during the recent ODI series, Zimbabwe's talented batter Kelis Ndhlovu was struck by a bouncer from Australian bowler Illing. The incident raised alarms among fans and teammates alike, prompting immediate medical attention. As the cricket community holds its breath, we delve into the details surrounding this incident and its implications for the Zimbabwean team.

## Incident Overview: Ndhlovu's Scary Moment

The match, held at the picturesque Harare Sports Club, saw Ndhlovu facing a fierce delivery from Illing that unfortunately struck her on the helmet. The impact was severe enough to warrant a quick medical intervention, with the Zimbabwean coaching staff and medical team rushing to her side. 

In the aftermath of the incident, Zimbabwe coach Kate Ebrahim provided an update on Ndhlovu's condition. “She’s responding now, which is really lovely to hear,” Ebrahim stated, offering a glimmer of hope to fans and teammates concerned about the young batter's health.

# Durham Sign Kemar Roach for First Half of Championship Season

In a significant move for the upcoming cricket season, Durham County Cricket Club has announced the signing of West Indies fast bowler Kemar Roach. The 34-year-old seamer, who has enjoyed a successful five-year association with Surrey, will join Durham for the first half of the County Championship season. This signing is expected to bolster Durham's bowling attack as they aim for a strong performance in the 2024 season.

## Kemar Roach: A Proven Performer

Kemar Roach is no stranger to the rigors of international and domestic cricket. With over 250 wickets in Test cricket and a wealth of experience in limited-overs formats, Roach brings a level of expertise that is invaluable to any team. His ability to swing the ball at pace, combined with his knack for taking crucial wickets, makes him a formidable opponent on the field.

During his time with Surrey, Roach played a pivotal role in their bowling lineup, helping the team secure vital victories and maintain competitive performances in the County Championship. His departure from Surrey marks the end of a fruitful partnership, but it also opens a new chapter in his career with Durham.

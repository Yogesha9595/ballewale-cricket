# Washington Picked Over Axar with an Eye on 'Winning the Powerplay', Says Ten Doeschate

In a surprising turn of events during the ICC Cricket World Cup 2023, the Indian team opted to leave out seasoned all-rounder Axar Patel in favor of Washington Sundar for their crucial match against South Africa. This decision came under scrutiny after India suffered a 76-run defeat, raising questions about the team’s strategy and selection process. In a post-match interview, assistant coach Ryan ten Doeschate shed light on this tactical decision, emphasizing the importance of 'winning the powerplay' in modern-day cricket.

## The Powerplay Dilemma

The powerplay overs are critical in limited-overs cricket, often setting the tone for the rest of the innings. Ten Doeschate explained that the decision to include Washington Sundar was primarily based on his ability to contribute significantly during these initial overs. “We believe that winning the powerplay is crucial in ODIs, especially against a formidable side like South Africa. Washington’s skill set allows us to be aggressive with the bat while also providing an option with the ball,” he stated.

Sundar, known for his off-spin bowling and ability to play aggressive shots, was seen as a player who could potentially change the dynamics of the game in the early stages. However, the gamble did not pay off as India struggled to find their rhythm, leading to a disappointing performance.

## Axar Patel: A Missed Opportunity?

Axar Patel’s omission raised eyebrows among fans and cricket analysts alike. The left-arm spinner has been a consistent performer for India, especially in the subcontinent conditions. His ability to control the game and pick up crucial wickets has made him a valuable asset. Ten Doeschate addressed this concern, stating, “Axar is an exceptional player, and his experience is invaluable. However, we felt that the conditions and the nature of the opposition called for a different approach.”

Despite the rationale behind the decision, many believe that Axar's presence could have made a difference, especially in the middle overs where South Africa capitalized on India’s bowling attack.

## Analyzing the Defeat

India's defeat against South Africa was not solely due to the team selection. The batting order struggled to build partnerships, and the bowlers failed to contain the opposition’s explosive batting lineup. South Africa posted a formidable total, and India’s chase fell short, leading to questions about the team’s overall strategy.

Ten Doeschate acknowledged the challenges faced by the team, stating, “We need to regroup and analyze our performance. It’s crucial to learn from this defeat and make the necessary adjustments moving forward.” The coaching staff is keen on addressing the issues that surfaced during the match to ensure a more cohesive performance in the upcoming games.

## Looking Ahead: The Road to Redemption

As India prepares for their next match in the World Cup, the focus will undoubtedly shift to team selection and strategy. The debate over whether to reinstate Axar Patel or continue with Washington Sundar will be a hot topic among fans and analysts. Ten Doeschate hinted at potential changes, saying, “We will assess the conditions and the opposition before finalizing our lineup. Every match is a new opportunity to showcase our strengths.”

The upcoming fixtures are crucial for India as they aim to bounce back from this setback. The team will need to harness their skills and experience to remain competitive in the tournament. Fans will be eagerly awaiting the announcement of the playing XI for the next match, hoping to see a balanced side that can effectively tackle the challenges ahead.

## Conclusion

The decision to pick Washington Sundar over Axar Patel against South Africa has sparked discussions about strategy and team dynamics in the World Cup 2023. While the powerplay remains a pivotal aspect of the game, the overall performance of the team will determine their success in the tournament. As India looks to redeem themselves, the cricketing world will be watching closely to see how they adapt and evolve in the face of adversity.

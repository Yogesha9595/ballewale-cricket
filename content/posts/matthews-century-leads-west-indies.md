# Matthews' Century Leads West Indies to Six-Wicket Victory

In a thrilling encounter at the picturesque Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, West Indies captain Stafanie Matthews showcased her all-round prowess, leading her team to a resounding six-wicket victory over Sri Lanka. This match was pivotal, as it prevented the visitors from clinching the series 3-0. Matthews' outstanding performance not only highlighted her leadership skills but also reignited the spirits of the West Indies women's cricket team.

## Matthews Shines with Bat and Ball

Stafanie Matthews was the star of the show, exhibiting a blend of aggression and composure. After winning the toss and opting to field first, Matthews took two crucial wickets, helping to restrict Sri Lanka to a modest total. Her bowling was sharp, and she consistently troubled the Sri Lankan batters with her variations. This early breakthrough laid the foundation for West Indies' successful chase.

When it was time for the bat, Matthews stepped up to the plate, scoring a magnificent century that was instrumental in guiding her team to victory. Her innings of 102 runs came off just 85 balls and was laced with 12 boundaries. The captain's ability to rotate the strike while finding the boundary made her innings a masterclass in batting under pressure.

## Sri Lanka's Struggles with the Bat

Sri Lanka, looking to secure a series whitewash, struggled to find their rhythm against a disciplined West Indies bowling attack. Despite a promising start, they faltered in the middle overs. The team managed to post a total of 225 runs, with their top-order batters failing to convert starts into big scores. The West Indies bowlers, led by Matthews, maintained tight lines and lengths, making scoring difficult for the Sri Lankan batswomen.

The standout performer for Sri Lanka was their captain, who managed to score a resilient 65 runs. However, her efforts were not enough to propel the team to a competitive total, as wickets fell at regular intervals. The West Indies bowlers, particularly Matthews, capitalized on the pressure, ensuring that Sri Lanka could not build any significant partnerships.

## West Indies' Chase: A Team Effort
